# Concept B decision log

Concept: Editorial Academic Product

| Choice | Governing source | Why necessary | Alternatives rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary content width is 1200 px with a maximum 1180 px Hero product frame. | Design Contract grid; Concept B Hero blueprint | The blueprint specifies 1120–1200 px, and 1180 px leaves a neutral inset inside the 1200 px grid. | Full 1280 px wide media; a smaller frame that reduces readability |
| Hero content is centered on desktop and left-aligned below 720 px. | Concept B Hero blueprint | The blueprint permits centered desktop and explicitly requires left-aligned mobile. | Concept A split Hero; centered mobile paragraphs |
| Hero headline uses the shared 72 px maximum and 900 px width. | Design Contract type range; Concept B 900 px maximum | This preserves the locked headline as two lines at desktop. | Type above 72 px; width beyond 900 px; decorative line breaks |
| Warm light-neutral is used only behind the Hero product frame. | Concept B Hero blueprint; approved light neutral | The blueprint explicitly limits the surface to the frame area. | Full Hero color field; gradient; dark product canvas |
| Problem statement and support use 7 columns and 4 columns, with a one-column gap. | Concept B Problem blueprint | This is the exact editorial proportion. | Equal split; timeline cards; centered copy |
| “Learning happens” is centered typographically on the minimal rule. | Concept B Problem blueprint | Typography provides the required emphasis without effects. | Color glow, pill, illustration, or icon |
| Editorial markers use a 1 px vertical rule, 14 px number, and 16 px label. | Concept B signature rules; supporting-label type range | The smallest functional marker remains readable without competing with content. | Large numerals, colored tiles, Concept C workflow index |
| Spreads alternate at desktop, but all mobile stages use marker, copy, product. | Concept B walkthrough blueprint | The blueprint explicitly prohibits reversing Stage 2 on mobile. | Mechanical alternating mobile order; product-first mobile stage |
| Stage spacing is 128 px desktop and 88 px mobile. | Design Contract spacing scale; Concept B editorial spreads | The spacing separates spreads without background bands. | Background changes for every stage; tighter card-like rhythm |
| Four positioning points use a four-column ruled row, changing to two columns at tablet and one at mobile. | Concept B Why blueprint | The blueprint allows a single row or two-by-two, and the breakpoints preserve readability. | Enclosing cards; icon grid; competitor matrix |
| Institutional content is a single low-contrast block with a teal left rule. | Concept B blueprint; Design Contract institutional hierarchy | A contained block makes review information secondary. | Trust theater, certifications, logos, dark band |
| Pilot conclusion remains white with a 1 px dark top rule. | Concept B CTA blueprint | The blueprint calls for an editorial conclusion and no required colored background. | Concept A contained light panel; Concept C expectation sequence |
| Borders and shadows are limited to placeholder framing, editorial rules, and the institutional block. | Design Contract surface rules | The prototype needs structure without becoming a card template. | Borders around every text group; glassmorphism; decorative shadows |
| Student placeholder becomes the approved taller mobile crop; setup and signals become single-column neutral frames. | Placeholder Spec; Concept B mobile rules | Product evidence remains meaningful near its stage copy. | Hiding screens, using tabs, or reducing frames below readable size |

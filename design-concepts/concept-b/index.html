Why MyTA

Built around the assignment, not another open-ended chat.

Publisher AI begins with publisher content. LMS AI spans the platform. Standalone tutors begin with a student question. MyTA begins with your assignment and brings the learning loop back to you.

Your course context

Your materials, learning goals, expectations, and approved references shape the experience.

Your rules for support

You decide where AI can help and where the student must work independently.

Active student thinking

The experience is intended to prompt attempts, explanations, checks, and revision.

Useful next steps

Learning signals are organized around decisions you can make as an instructor.

Start with one course. Review the fit before scaling.

MyTA is intended to work alongside existing LMS workflows. A focused pilot gives faculty and university teams a practical way to evaluate teaching value, student experience, privacy, accessibility, security, and integration needs before any broader decision.

Designed to work alongside systems such as Canvas, Brightspace, Moodle, and other LMS platforms.

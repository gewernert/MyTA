Instructor control

Your course, your rules.

Instructors shape the context, expectations, and support boundaries. Students remain responsible for their reasoning and final response.

Course context
Approved slides, notes, examples, and readings.
Assignment intent
Learning objectives, expectations, and what students should demonstrate.
Support boundaries
When AI may ask, hint, explain, reference, or check understanding.

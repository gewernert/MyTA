The instructor problem

You see the assignment. You see the submission.

The learning in between is mostly invisible. Students use AI, get stuck, seek help, revise, and build understanding before a final answer ever reaches the instructor.

Instructor control

Learning design

Designed to make students think.

Support is structured around retrieval, explanation, staged hints, and reflection.

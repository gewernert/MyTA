Instructor assignment creation

Instructor control

Your course, your rules.

Instructors shape each assignment's context, expectations, and support boundaries. MyTA is structured around the course, not a generic chat window.

Course context
Approved slides, notes, examples, and readings.
Assignment intent
Learning objectives, expectations, and what students should demonstrate.
Support boundaries
When AI may ask, hint, explain, reference, or check understanding.
Human review
AI can be wrong. Students own their response; instructors own teaching decisions.

Learning design

Designed to make students think.

Support is informed by established learning principles: retrieval, self-explanation, staged scaffolding, and reflection.

  1. 01
    Ask before answerPrompt recall or reasoning before offering an explanation.
  2. 02
    Hint in stagesProvide enough support to keep the work moving.
  3. 03
    Explain and reflectAsk students to justify steps and revisit revisions.
  4. 04
    Fade the supportReduce scaffolding as understanding develops.

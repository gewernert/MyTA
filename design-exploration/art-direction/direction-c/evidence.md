# Direction C: Continuous assignment story

## Reference roles

- Granola: lifecycle continuity and stable narrative logic.
- Gradescope: familiar course-workflow clarity.
- IgniteAI: instructor control and calm adoption confidence.

## Borrowed

- One continuous lifecycle rather than repeated feature cards.
- A recognizable assignment object and practical sequence.
- Instructor control as the beginning and end of the story.

## Rejected

- Granola's meeting metaphor and brand styling.
- Gradescope's grading and analytics-centered workflow.
- IgniteAI's enterprise breadth, trust cards, and compliance language.

## Workflow communication

One folded assignment surface transforms from instructor context into active student reasoning, gathers learning signals, and turns back toward an instructor action zone. The sequence is spatial rather than a stepper or box-and-arrow diagram.

## Provisional

The continuous surface is a storytelling device. Product validation would determine which real interface states replace its abstract regions.

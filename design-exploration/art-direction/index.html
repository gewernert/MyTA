Evidence notes and isolated prototypes

Direction A: Interface authority

Reference roles: Linear for precision and product scale; Class Companion for immediate assignment comprehension; Turnitin Clarity for assignment-specific evidence.

Borrowed: One dominant product composition and a direct relationship between student work and instructor evidence.

Rejected: Dark SaaS identity, K-12 tone, outcome claims, detection, and surveillance framing.

Workflow: Instructor context sits left, student reasoning dominates the center, and signals return on the right.

Provisional: Abstract interface content would be replaced with validated product states.

Direction B: Education-first expression

Reference roles: Google for Education for an education-specific environment; OpenAI Education for calm hierarchy; Linear for restraint.

Borrowed: An education-first visual world, controlled hierarchy, and one large anchor.

Rejected: Reference-brand colors and identities, campus-wide claims, and generic SaaS dashboards.

Workflow: The assignment is a shared folio where context enters, reasoning develops, and signals collect in the margin.

Provisional: The folio is an art-direction metaphor rather than a literal interface.

Open Direction B · Full evidence note

Direction C: Continuous assignment story

Reference roles: Granola for lifecycle continuity; Gradescope for familiar course-workflow clarity; IgniteAI for control and adoption confidence.

Borrowed: One continuous lifecycle, a recognizable assignment object, and instructor control at both ends.

Rejected: Meeting metaphors, grading-centered analytics, enterprise breadth, and trust-card layouts.

Workflow: One folded assignment surface transforms from instructor intent to student work to signals and instructor action.

Provisional: Product validation would determine which real states replace the abstract regions.

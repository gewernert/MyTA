# Direction B: Education-first brand expression

## Reference roles

- Google for Education: education-specific visual identity and promise-to-product continuity.
- OpenAI Education: calm institutional hierarchy.
- Linear: restraint and product authority.

## Borrowed

- A visual world grounded in education rather than generic SaaS.
- Calm hierarchy and controlled information density.
- One large visual anchor instead of a grid of features.

## Rejected

- Google's colors, ecosystem breadth, and environmental imagery.
- OpenAI's dark institutional identity, logos, and campus-wide claims.
- Linear's dark product-development aesthetic.

## Workflow communication

The assignment appears as a shared folio. Course sources enter at the top, student reasoning occupies the center, and instructor-facing signals gather along the margin, making the assignment itself the unit of design.

## Provisional

The folio is an art-direction metaphor, not a literal product interface. Its abstract content avoids invented features and claims.

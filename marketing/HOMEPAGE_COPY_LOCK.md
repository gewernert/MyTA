## Approved objection responses

### Is this another chatbot?

MyTA is being built around your assignment, your materials, your learning goals, and your support rules. The product focus is the connected assignment workflow, not open-ended chat.

### Will it give students the answer?

The intended experience prompts students to attempt, explain, check, and revise. Students remain responsible for the final response. MyTA should not be described as preventing every shortcut.

### What if the AI is wrong?

Instructor-approved context can make support more relevant, but it does not guarantee accuracy. Student responsibility, checks for understanding, and instructor review remain important.

### Will this create more work?

A pilot should begin with one assignment, a small amount of approved course material, and a focused setup conversation. Do not promise that it requires no instructor work.

### Is this another disconnected platform?

MyTA is intended to work alongside existing LMS workflows rather than replace them. Do not claim completed integrations unless they are verified.

### Is this student surveillance?

The purpose is to surface useful learning signals for support and instructional action. Avoid language about tracking every action, policing students, or proving misconduct.

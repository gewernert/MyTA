## Baseline findings

- Display size is interpolated to 75.6 px at 1440, 67.2 px at 1280, and 52.736 px at 1024 rather than resolving to a named role value.
- Hero supporting copy falls to 15 px at 1024 and below.
- Desktop Hero and Section 2 eyebrows use 0.075em and 0.16em tracking respectively; mobile Hero uses 0.07em.
- Mobile chapter titles are 27 px while the mobile Section 2 headline is 34 to 38 px, leaving insufficient separation between section and chapter hierarchy.
- Mobile boundary-to-Section 2 eyebrow spacing is 38 px at normal-flow breakpoints.
- Desktop semantic gaps already fall inside the requested ranges and should be preserved while being expressed through tokens.

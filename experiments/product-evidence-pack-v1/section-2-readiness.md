# Section 2 readiness

## Decision: READY WITH LIMITS

The real product can support most of the intended assignment journey:

1. Instructor sets boundaries: partially supported by the real Course Material screen, which proves course context and approved references.
2. Student works: strongly supported by the real assignment workspace.
3. MyTA guides: strongly supported by the real Hint interaction and the verified Hint, Explain concept, and Check reasoning actions.
4. Instructor acts: partially supported by the teacher dashboard's weekly briefing and Needs grading queue.

## Limits that must shape Section 2

- Do not depict or claim a complete instructor configuration layer. No verified screen exposed learning goals, assignment expectations, AI support rules, or checks for understanding.
- Do not present the teacher weekly briefing as validated outcomes or live institutional analytics. It is seeded product evidence.
- Do not claim a completed instructor intervention workflow. The inspected teacher dashboard identifies work needing attention, but a real submission-review or assignment-adjustment destination was not verified.
- Do not use cohort proportions or other seeded values as homepage proof points.
- Treat mobile as a sequential single-pane experience; the problem and MyTA support are not visible together.

## Evidence still needed for an unrestricted Section 2

- A verified assignment-setup screen showing instructor-controlled support boundaries.
- A verified assignment-level signals screen tying student help behavior to a specific assignment or concept.
- A verified instructor action destination, such as real submission review, targeted support, or assignment adjustment.
- Exact 1440 × 1000 recaptures of the five student desktop surfaces from a browser environment that returns the full requested viewport.
- A documented local run command or local source location for the authenticated product application.

Until those states exist or are captured, Section 2 can truthfully show the real student workflow and use the teacher surfaces as limited supporting evidence, but it should not visually bridge the missing controls or actions with invented UI.

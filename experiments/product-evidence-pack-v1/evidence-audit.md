# Evidence audit

## A. Instructor control — FOUND WITH LIMITS

- Exact supporting screen: Teacher Course Material at `/teacher/course-knowledge`.
- Screenshot: `evidence/01-instructor-control-1440x1000.png`.
- Truthful claim: An instructor can organize course-specific references by class and material type, including lecture notes, references, rubrics, and instructor-only solutions.
- Cannot claim: Verified learning-goal controls, assignment expectations, AI support rules, checks for understanding, or a complete instructor boundary-setting workflow.
- Visual quality: Clear, calm, and populated. The upload form and real materials establish course context, but this is reference management rather than a full assignment-control screen.
- Public website suitability: Suitable as supporting evidence for course context or approved references, with careful labeling.

## B. Student assignment workspace — FOUND

- Exact supporting screen: Problem Set 5 assignment workspace in two-page mode.
- Screenshot: `evidence/02-student-assignment-1440x1000.png`.

- Cannot claim: Instructor-configured behavior, learning outcomes, grading accuracy, or any feature not visible in the screen.

## D. Instructor learning signals — FOUND WITH LIMITS

- Screenshot: `evidence/04-instructor-signals-1440x1000.png`.
